Welcome to Berlin!

My name is Dennis Behnke, I am the Berlin Storyteller. If you are looking for a private tour in Berlin, you have come to the right place!

Whether you are looking for a tour about the highlights of Berlin, want to know more about Berlin's history of the Third Reich, get an insight into the life in the divided city beyond the Berlin Wall or simply want to explore the modern Berlin, coming with me on a private tour is your best option.

I am a Berlin native, born in what used to be the American sector of Berlin during the last years of the division. Since 2007 I have been guiding guests through this town, giving tours with a wide variety of topics and places. My personal fascination with Berlin's past, supported by my degree in History and German Literature of the University of Potsdam have made me the ideal guide for everyone looking for an unique insider's view of German history and identity.

Be it on foot and public transportation, be it on bike or in a van, let me help you create your perfect Berlin private tour.

Dennis
