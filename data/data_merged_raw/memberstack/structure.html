How Memberstack Works

Build anything in Webflow , and use Memberstack to monetize it 🤑

When it comes to design and branding, you're the boss - build anything you want in Webflow, and use custom attributes to make it work with Memberstack.

Explore all features Open demo

Sign Up & Log In Forms

Use Webflow to design your sign up and log in forms, then make them work using easy-to-apply custom attributes in Webflow - no code needed!

Passwordless Login

Give your members the gift of one less password to remember. Add a few more custom attributes and let your members sign up with a code sent via email.

10+ Social Auth Integrations

Depending on what you're building, it might make sense for your members to sign up with Google, Facebook, or any other of our easy, native social auth integrations.

Gate Entire Pages & Specific Elements

With no code and just a few clicks, you can restrict access to content so that only users with the right level of access can view it. Set custom URL rules, use custom attributes for Webflow elements, and send users to your own custom access denied page.

Display User Data

Give your members a personalized experience by displaying their data back to them - with just one attribute.

Accept 40+ Global Payment Methods

Memberstack works on top of Stripe - the leading online payment processor - meaning you can accept over 40 different payment methods, including globally used methods Apple Pay, and local methods like iDEAL.

Developer Friendly

Our Admin API & DOM Package makes Memberstack developer-friendly.

Multi-Step Onboarding Flows

With Memberstack, you can build a fully custom onboarding flow with as many steps as you want. Anything you can build in Webflow, you can use Memberstack to make it work.

Billing & Pricing
Does Memberstack have a free trial?

Yes, absolutely! All of Memberstack's features are free to try (no credit card) on a test site for an unlimited amount of time . This means that you can take the time you need to set up Memberstack fully in test mode, and only pay when your website is ready to accept real members.

How do the member limits work?

We count the total number of Live members. This includes all free and paying members. And excludes any test mode members.

You will receive an email when you are 90% of the way to your current member limit.
‍
When you reach 100% on the Basic and Professional plans we will automatically upgrade your account to the next tier. If you are on the Business or Established plans we will automatically increase your member limit by 20,000 to avoid interruptions.

What are transaction fees and when do they apply?

We define a transaction as whenever a member pays to sign up on your website through Memberstack. A transaction fee is a service fee based on a given percentage of each transaction.

If you only have free members, then the transaction fees do not apply to you. If you do require your members to pay during signup, then Memberstack charges a flat transaction fee based on your plan.

Please note that Memberstack's transaction fees do not include the fees that Stripe charges.

How do I receive the money from the sales on my Website?

Memberstack uses Stripe to process payments securely on our users' websites and for payouts. Money you earn on your website is directly and securely transferred to your bank account via Stripe.

Data & Security
Does Memberstack have a privacy policy & security policy?

Yes. Our Terms of Service , Privacy Policy , Security Policy , Data Processing Addendum , Cookie Policy and List of Sub-processors are accessible at the bottom of any page on our website.

Are my members' emails and passwords secured by Memberstack?

Yes. Data stored in Memberstack about your members is secured using industry best practices — this includes member emails, passwords and metadata. We force HTTPS so that data between your website and our servers is always encrypted . Data stored in our database is also encrypted at rest. We also take regular automated backups in order to stay safe.

We are an official Stripe Partner to process payments — so we do not have access to and do not store any financial information .

Does Memberstack access or store any of my members' financial information?

Memberstack uses Stripe to process payments — we are an Official Stripe Partner. We do not have access to and do not store any financial information about our customer's members.

Can I use Memberstack to gate highly sensitive information?

Most Memberstack features gate content on the front-end - meaning, a determined person with the right knowledge will be able to get around our content gating. This applies to pages/elements that you build in Webflow, and then gate using Memberstack.

However, there are various ways to keep information secure, like:
‍
1. Hosted Content: Using our hosted content feature, you can keep text-based content (including links & HTML) stored inside of Memberstack, and it is not served to the user unless they have been authenticated.
2. Member JSON & Metadata: This member-specific information is added by the member and lives inside Memberstack. The only person who should be able to access it is the owner of the account, and the account admin.

Security is a tricky and nuanced topic - if you're unsure if Memberstack's security is a good fit for your use case, please send a message in our Slack channel to get advice and recommendations from the community, our team, and our experts.

Is Memberstack GDPR compliant?

Yes — We are committed to helping our customers and users understand and, where applicable, comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In addition to strengthening and standardizing user data privacy across jurisdictions, GDPR introduce additional obligations on all organizations that handle personal data.

On this page, we explain how Memberstack complies with GDPR.
