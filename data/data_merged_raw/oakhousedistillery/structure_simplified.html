OUR STORY

Oak House Distillery, founded in 2020 by UGA scientists, is Athens' first legal distillery since the 1860s. We craft award-winning gins and rums in a historic setting with a welcoming atmosphere. Enjoy cocktails, tours, and exceptional service. Visit our online shop.
