my story

Hi, I'm Noå. My athletic journey began at 6, kayaking in The Netherlands. I progressed to canoe polo and became a coach at various levels. This experience laid the foundation for my work as a performance coach.

I'm passionate about lifelong learning to enhance quality of life and performance through awareness and exercise.

I enjoy organizing breathing sessions, cold water experiences, and leadership trails in The Netherlands and abroad. I look forward to connecting and creating challenges together!
