India’s e-waste underworld

India is drowning in its own discarded gadgets. From smartphones to servers, our e-waste pile is now the third largest in the world – and it’s only getting bigger. Is it too big an ask for India to handle this mounting pile of toxic waste?

We aim to uncover the story behind India’s broken e-waste system, where environmental concerns intersect with corporate fraud. It is an investigation that digs into the shady economics of recycling, the regulatory chaos that enables illegal dumping, and the informal sector that handles the country’s digital deadweight. Who profits, who suffers, and who looks the other way?

To follow the trail, we’ll travel to five states, including one in South India. Contribute to this NL Sena project to help us expose the toxic truths behind India’s e-waste crisis.
