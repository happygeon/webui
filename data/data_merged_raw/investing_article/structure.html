Who Founded Apple?
Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne. Wayne sold his share of the company back to Jobs and Wozniak just 12 days after the company's founding.

Where is Apple HQ?
While this company has operations around the globe, the current AAPL headquarters is located in Cupertino, California (U.S.) at Apple Park, a circular "spaceship" campus that opened in 2017.

When Did Apple Stock Go Public?
Apple went public on December 12, 1980. The IPO price was $22 per share, generating more capital than any IPO since Ford Motor Company in 1956 and instantly creating more millionaires (about 300) than any company in history up to that point.

When Was the Last Time Apple Stock Split?
Apple's most recent stock split occurred on August 31, 2020. This was a 4-for-1 stock split. Previously, Apple had three 2-for-1 splits (1987, 2000, 2005) and a 7-for-1 split in 2014.

What Stock Exchange Does Apple Trade On?
Apple Inc is listed and trades on the NASDAQ stock exchange. It was originally listed on the NASDAQ in 1980 and has remained there since.

What Is the Stock Symbol for Apple?
The stock symbol for Apple Inc is "AAPL."

Is AAPL the Same as $AAPL?
$AAPL is a common way to refer to the Apple Inc ticker symbol in social media, forums, and online discussions. The dollar sign is a shorthand convention used to indicate that it is a stock ticker symbol.

What Are Apple's Main Product Categories?
Apple's main product categories include the iPhone (which generates the majority of revenue), Mac computers, iPad tablets, wearables (Apple Watch, AirPods), home products, and services (including the App Store, Apple Music, Apple TV+, iCloud, and Apple Pay).

Who Is Apple's Current CEO?
Tim Cook has been Apple's CEO since August 2011, succeeding founder Steve Jobs who passed away in October 2011. Before becoming CEO, Cook served as Apple's Chief Operating Officer and was responsible for the company's worldwide operations.

What Makes Apple One of the Most Valuable Companies?
Apple has achieved its position as one of the world's most valuable companies through its innovative products, loyal customer base, high-margin business model, ecosystem integration, strong brand recognition, and substantial cash reserves. Apple was the first company to reach a $1 trillion market capitalization (in 2018) and later the first to reach $2 trillion and $3 trillion.

Apple Inc Company Profile

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories worldwide. The company offers iPhone, a line of smartphones; Mac, a line of personal computers; iPad, a line of multi-purpose tablets; and wearables, home, and accessories comprising AirPods, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Beats products, and HomePod. It also provides AppleCare support and cloud services; and operates various platforms, including the App Store that allow customers to discover and download applications and digital content, such as books, music, video, games, and podcasts, as well as advertising services include third-party licensing arrangements and its own advertising platforms. In addition, the company offers various subscription-based services, such as Apple Arcade, a game subscription service; Apple Fitness+, a personalized fitness service; Apple Music, which offers users a curated listening experience with on-demand radio stations; Apple News+, a subscription news and magazine service; Apple TV+, which offers exclusive original content; Apple Card, a co-branded credit card; and Apple Pay, a cashless payment service, as well as licenses its intellectual property. The company serves consumers, and small and mid-sized businesses; and the education, enterprise, and government markets. It distributes third-party applications for its products through the App Store. The company also sells its products through its retail and online stores, and direct sales force; and third-party cellular network carriers, wholesalers, retailers, and resellers. Apple Inc. was founded in 1976 and is headquartered in Cupertino, California.
