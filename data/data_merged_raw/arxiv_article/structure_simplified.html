Title: Decision Transformer: Reinforcement Learning via Sequence Modeling

Authors: Lili Chen, Kevin Lu, Aravind Rajeswaran, Kimin Lee, Aditya Grover, Michael Laskin, Pieter Abbeel, Aravind Srinivas, Igor Mordatch
View PDF
Abstract: We present a framework that treats Reinforcement Learning (RL) as a sequence modeling issue, utilizing the Transformer architecture. Our Decision Transformer outputs optimal actions based on desired returns, past states, and actions, achieving performance comparable to state-of-the-art RL methods on various tasks.
