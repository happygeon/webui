Start Your Crowdfunding Journey With Ketto

Ketto: Leading Crowdfunding and Fundraising Website in India

Are you familiar with the concept of online crowdfunding? India, over the last few years, has seen a number of crowdfunding platforms come up.

In India, crowdfunding is not a new concept. During the olden days, people would go door to door asking their neighbours for monetary help. When it comes to fundraising in India , this method was time-consuming, but those who were raising funds were able to create a personal bond with those who were helping them. They would visit their neighbours, share their stories and explain why they needed funds. Today, there are a number of crowdfunding websites in India, and people can raise funds online.

What is Ketto?

Do you want to start a crowdfunding campaign or crowdfund ? India has a number of crowdfunding websites on which you can start a fundraiser. Indian crowdfunding platforms help the campaigner connect to a wider audience and raise funds smoothly. These fundraising websites also have features like easy fundraiser creation, secure payments and marketing tools, which allow you to reach your fundraising goals.

Ketto Online Ventures Pvt. Ltd. is an online crowdfunding platform in India that helps individuals and organisations in the country raise funds for a cause that means something to them. It doesn’t charge any platform fee*, which makes it easier for campaigners, i.e. people who have started a fundraiser, to raise funds online . Whether you need funds for medical care, or to support an NGO, Ketto can be your go-to website to raise funds .

What Can You Fundraise Online For?

You can raise funds online for a number of causes on Ketto. What is the most common cause or fundraiser for which people raise money? Medical fundraising is the most common, which helps patients with cancer treatment, heart disease, kidney failure, and more. You can also support NGOs focused on environmental conservation, child rights, women's rights, poverty, and human rights. We connect generous people with those who need help.

Beginnings

Founded in 2012 by Varun Seth, Kunal Kapoor and Zaheer Adenwala, Ketto is a crowdfunding platform based in India. Ketto has helped countless individuals and organisations in raising funds since it began.

Ketto Meaning : The word 'Ketto' means 'Key To Tomorrow,' and we live by our mission to help people get the resources they need for better health and happiness.

Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to connect people to access healthcare and share happiness. Our vision is “Healthcare for All.” We believe that it is important for everyone to get access to necessary medical care despite their financial situation.

Ketto SIP

Ketto SIP : Ketto SIP is an initiative we started wherein supporters can contribute as little as Rs 100 a month to a cause close to their hearts. They can support fundraisers and contribute towards a child's education or help feed underprivileged children.

How Can You Start a Fundraiser on Ketto or How To Raise Funds On Ketto:

Starting a crowdfunding campaign on www.ketto.org is an easy 4 step process :

1. Create an Account: Visit www.ketto.org and set up a fundraising account.
2. Create Your Campaign: Provide information about your cause, add visual elements like images or videos, and establish a fundraising target.
3. Promote Your Campaign: You can share your fundraising campaign on Facebook, Instagram, Whatsapp or any other social media platforms or through email so that you can reach more people.
4. Engage with Supporters: Update your supporters regularly and thank them for their contributions.

Ketto provides a platform for friends and family to come together and help the bereaved family during this difficult time.

FAQs

Q. How could I be sure that my contribution will reach the person for whom the fundraiser has been started?

A. Once you have contributed towards a fundraiser, you will receive updates through Email/SMS/WhatsApp over the following months about the fundraiser that you supported. Alternatively, you can go to the fundraiser page and message the person who has started the fundraiser through the 'Contact Button' option.

Q. If I make a contribution towards a fundraiser, what steps does Ketto follow to protect my identity and payment information?

A. At Ketto, we give utmost importance to your safety. We understand how important your personal information is to you, and that is why we take every measure to protect it. Ketto uses high-security measures, including 128-bit encryption, to keep your information safe. We also never share your personal details without your permission.

Q. What do you mean by ‘Ketto Assured’?

A. Imagine you are scrolling through Ketto, and you come across a medical fundraiser you wish to contribute to. You will see an ‘ Assured Protection ’ batch on the same. This batch means that the Ketto team has personally verified that the fundraiser is authentic and credible and that your contribution reaches the patient who is undergoing treatment. The Ketto team thoroughly verifies the fundraisers on our platform to ensure they are real . When you contribute to fundraisers on our platform, we will ensure that the amount you contribute reaches the person or beneficiaries the fundraiser was started for.

Q. How can I start a fundraiser on Ketto for non-medical reasons, like education or memorial?

A. Visit our site, www.ketto.org and then click on the 'Start a Fundraiser' button. After doing this, since you don't want to start a medical fundraiser, change your purpose of starting the fundraiser from 'Medical Treatment' to 'Other Cause', and after this, you can submit the form.
