Immanuel Wilderness Lodge

Best Accommodation in Windhoek | #1 on Tripadvisor

Conveniently located just minutes from Windhoek , the Immanuel Wilderness Lodge offers an oasis of tranquility in the magnificent Namibian Savannah.

Immanuel Wilderness Lodge is renowned for its fantastic food. The lodge and Restaurant is owned by German expatriates Sabine and Stephan , an acclaimed, Chaîne des Rôtisseurs Award winning chef.

This comfortable, family-run lodge makes an ideal base for exploring the remarkable surrounding wilderness. Traditional thatched roofs and rustic details create an authentically African atmosphere while nine lovely rooms with ensuite bathrooms add all the comforts of home.

Revitalise yourself in the refreshing pool and indulge in some hearty meals served by the lodge’s award-winning chef, or join our daily bush activities .

If you are looking for a truly Namibian experience complete with personal service and warm hospitality, look no further than the exceptional Immanuel Wilderness Lodge.
