IFRC’s World Refugee Day message: ‘We are with you’

Imagine being a mother of two, fleeing armed conflict to save your life. This is the story of Munana, who escaped from Sudan to Chad, receiving support from the Red Cross.

As we mark World Refugee Day 2025, the IFRC calls for solidarity with refugees through action and support for our humanitarian work.

Solidarity is crucial as millions are displaced due to violence, with over 12.3 million people forced from their homes in Sudan alone.

More than 165 National Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies are working to support refugees and migrants globally, providing essential services and assistance.

Support continues even after refugees arrive in new communities, helping them settle and access necessary services.

Numerous National Societies offer mental health support and language classes to help refugees integrate into new environments.
