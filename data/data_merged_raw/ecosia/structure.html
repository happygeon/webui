Why choose Ecosia?

What we stand for

  1. 100% of profits for the planet

    We use all our profits for climate action, with the majority going into tree-planting projects around the world.

  2. Collective climate action

    The Ecosia community is the planet's largest tree-planting movement, making a difference every day.

  3. No greenwashing

    We tell you transparently how much money we make each month, and how much we spend on tree planting and climate action.

  4. Beyond neutral

    Our solar panels produce enough energy to power your searches twice over, meaning more renewables (and fewer fossil fuels) in the energy grid.
