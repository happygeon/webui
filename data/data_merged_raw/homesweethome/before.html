HOME SWEET HOME, Bed and Breakfast &amp; Spa in Ciré d'Aunis

Our Charente house with century-old walls is a restored old farmhouse combining authentic charm and modernity: a real favorite for this little corner of paradise.
As soon as you step through the front door, you enter a peaceful place, with the only sound being that of nature and the Japanese pond fountain: enchanting.
Here we are out of time, perfect for relaxing.

Our three rooms are decorated and maintained with care, the gourmet breakfast is prepared with fresh, local products.

Located a few kilometers from the sea, our guest house is the ideal starting point for exploring La Rochelle, the sublime islands of Oléron, Aix and Ré, the Poitevin marsh, Rochefort, etc.

The advantages of the house : the lush garden, the spacious jacuzzi, the large swimming pool heated from April to September (weather permitting), the peace and quiet

Our little extras : personalized services (weddings, birthdays, etc.), tasting of specialties, dogs accepted on request
