Welcome to Majlis!

A team of women lawyers and activists working for women's and children's rights through legal representation and advocacy.

Majlis’ Rahat provides legal and social support to victims of violence. Majlis’ Flavia Agnes Centre promotes research and discourse on gender, law, and society.

Our Work

Rahat

Majlis’ Rahat offers support to women and children victims of violence, ensuring their dignity during legal processes.

Legal Support: We provide advice and representation in domestic and sexual violence cases.

Social Support: We assist victims in accessing medical care, shelter, and financial independence.

Flavia Agnes Centre

Majlis’ Flavia Agnes Centre is a hub for advancing knowledge on gender and law, focusing on curriculum development, research, training, and policy advocacy.

Where it all Started

Co-founder Flavia Agnes, a domestic violence survivor, started Majlis in 1991 to help others facing similar challenges.
