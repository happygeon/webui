I'm the author of several books including Things Become Other Things (SP 2023, Random House 2025) and Kissa by Kissa (SP 2020).

My writing chronicles my walks across Japan, my home since 2000.

I also write for The New York Times, The Atlantic, Eater, and other publications.

I've been a MacDowell, VCCA, Tin House, and Ragdale writing fellow.

My books blend photography, narrative non-fiction, and auto-fiction.

My rules: Lightness above weightiness, elevate everyone you encounter.
