HOW TO CARE FOR SLIME

  • What ages can enjoy slime?

    Slime can be enjoyed by children and adults alike. It offers a tactile and stress-relieving experience for individuals of all ages.

  • is slime edibile?

    No, Slime is absolutely not edible and should never be eaten.

    While our products may look delicious, they are not to be eaten under any circumstances.

  • how do I fix sticky slime?

    Slime can become sticky especially in hot weather or if it’s been sitting for a long time, this is fixable. Simply spray the slime with our handy Activator Pen (you can grab yours here if you don’t already have one). Stir until the liquid is mixed in completely, and test the slime. Is it still sticky? Repeat the process until the desired texture has been reached! Be careful not to use too much or your slime may get hard.

    If you need more activator, you can purchase it from our website, or make it from home! A big box of borax is $5 at Target or other grocery locations. Simply mix 1 cup hot water to 1 tablespoon borax, stir until dissolved.

  • My slime is not stretchy!

    Not to worry! Slimes can become especially stiff feeling in colder weather, or if they don't melt enough in hot weather.

    Some things you can use to fix this are lotion, hand sanitizer, hair gel, or microwaving it for a few seconds! These will all help it "melt" back down a bit to your desired texture.

    NOTE: do not add lotion to clear glue slime as it will make it foggy

    You can also leave it in a warm dry place while the slime softens.

  • What is the Activator Pen for?

    The Activator Pen (AKA our “Sticky Fix” Pen) is borax and water, used to make slime less sticky. Simply spray the slime with the Activator Pen once, stir until the liquid is mixed in completely, and test the slime. Is it still sticky? Repeat the process until the desired texture has been reached!

  • My slime is too stiff after spraying!

    Sounds like you added too much activator to your slime! No worries, just seal your slime up and leave in a warm, dry place until it softens. This process should take anywhere from a few days to a few months, depending on how overactivated the product is.
