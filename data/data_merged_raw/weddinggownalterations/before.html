    About Us

    Our Background

    We’re a mother/daughter team Serving Derbyshire, Burton-on-Trent, Nottingham, and clients in a 50-mile radius. We provide all of our alterations and bespoke services in house and as trained designers, we are able to carry out any pattern cutting and grading to ensure the highest quality continues throughout your dress. I started in the bridal business in 1989, and had the amazing experience of learning my trade in the exquisite Nottingham Lace Market. I trained in design, manufacture, and pattern cutting, and finished my designer qualification at Loughborough University. I became a designer for a large UK-based bridal wholesaler that services many countries throughout the world and now we make sure all brides have the bridal looking they are dreaming of.
