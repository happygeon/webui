2025 AP CFO Survey | Deloitte Korea

Asia Pacific CFOs face faster and more complex changes in the second half of 2025 than in previous periods. Deloitte’s latest CFO Survey shows that CFOs are evolving beyond simple financial managers to key decision makers at the center of a company’s strategic direction and sustainable growth. Key trends highlighted include innovation in the finance function through generative AI and automation, a shift in perspective from viewing ESG as a criterion for performance and investment decisions rather than costs, and CFOs establishing practical implementation strategies centered on securing digital talent and internalizing capabilities.
