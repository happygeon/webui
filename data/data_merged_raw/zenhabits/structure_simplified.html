How to Choose What to Do

By Leo Babauta

Choosing what to do can be overwhelming for many people.

Common obstacles include:

  • Too many tasks
  • Endless emails
  • Numerous browser tabs
  • Daily life responsibilities
  • Interruptions
  • Unplanned events
  • Pending projects

To simplify decision-making, follow these steps:

  1. Make a comprehensive list of tasks.
  2. Create a short list of 3–5 priorities based on your intuition.
  3. Focus on one task at a time.

Regularly update your long list and clear smaller tasks. At the end of each day, prepare your short list.

This approach helps you practice intuitive decision-making.

If you feel overwhelmed by choices, you're not alone.
