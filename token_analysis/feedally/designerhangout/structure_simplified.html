Catch & Release Fishing Guide Service - Belton Lake - Stillhouse Hollow Lake

Holding the Line Guide Service is a veteran-owned, full-time, catch-and-release fishing guide service operated by Bob Maindelle. We primarily fish on Belton Lake and Stillhouse Hollow Lake, conducting 170-220 guided trips per year.

Based in Salado, Texas, we cater to anglers from various nearby areas.

With thirty years of experience, we adapt our tactics based on fish location, using techniques like vertical jigging and live shad fishing.
