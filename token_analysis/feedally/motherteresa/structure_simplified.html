Meet, know, and love Mother Teresa of Calcutta

MOTHER TERESA WITH THE CHILDREN IN CALCUTTA SHISHUBHAVAN, 1980's.

Mother Teresa’s life showed the joy of loving and the dignity of every person. She founded the Missionaries of Charity to serve the poorest.

Born in 1910 in Skopje, Mother Teresa became a missionary in India. She was canonized in 2016. Her legacy continues through the Missionaries of Charity.
