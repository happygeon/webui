About Mother Teresa

Mother Teresa was born in Albania in 1910 and founded the Missionaries of Charity in 1950. She dedicated her life to serving the poor and marginalized, particularly those affected by HIV/AIDS. Known for her selflessness and compassion, she was canonized by Pope John Paul II in 1997.

Her Legacy

Mother Teresa's legacy continues through the Missionaries of Charity, which today operates in more than 130 countries. Her commitment to service and love has inspired countless individuals around the world.
