About Khan Academy

Khan Academy is a not-for-profit educational organization with the mission of providing a free, world-class education for anyone, anywhere. Our resources cover K-12 math & science, higher education, test prep, and more. We're supported by donors, grants, and our members who help us reach millions of learners worldwide.

Key Features

Accessibility-Friendly

Our platform supports screen readers and offers closed captions for videos to ensure accessibility for all users.

Personalized Learning

Adaptive quizzes and exercises adjust in difficulty based on individual performance, ensuring each learner progresses at their own pace.

Wide Range of Subjects

Covers everything from basic arithmetic to advanced physics and computer programming, catering to diverse learning needs.
