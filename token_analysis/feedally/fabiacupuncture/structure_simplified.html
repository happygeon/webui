About

I am from Brazil and transitioned from a corporate career to studying acupuncture after experiencing its benefits. I hold a BSc (Hons) and a license to practice Acupuncture from the College of Integrated Chinese Medicine in Reading, England. I also have diplomas in Tuina and Paediatrics Acupuncture and am a member of the British Acupuncture Council.

My practice focuses on treating the root causes of illnesses, not just symptoms, and I aim to help the body restore balance.
