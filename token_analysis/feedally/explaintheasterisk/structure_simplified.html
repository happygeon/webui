Explain The Asterisk

How do colleges handle students suspended for sexual violence or stalking?

THE PROBLEM

When a student is dismissed for sexual misconduct, their transcript often lacks clarity on the reason. Some schools use a small asterisk to indicate dismissal, but this can be overlooked. New schools may not know the full context of the dismissal, and many do not conduct background checks, relying instead on self-reporting.

OUR MISSION

Explain the Asterisk aims to end campus sexual violence by focusing on survivor experiences and advocating for policy reform at all levels.

OUR WORK

  • We center survivor experiences in policy reform.
  • New York and Virginia require institutions to note a "red flag" for dangerous dismissals.
  • The Safe Transfer Act was introduced to require all institutions to explain dismissals.
  • We advocate for disciplinary notations at federal and state levels.
  • Our mission includes ongoing advocacy to prevent sexual violence on campuses.
