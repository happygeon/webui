Automattic Twenty

June 20, 2025 Automattic Matt

We’re celebrating Automattic’s 20th anniversary with a look back at our journey.

In 2005, being a remote-first company was unusual, but we persevered thanks to early supporters.

Today, remote work is common, and I wonder what unique practices we have now that will become standard.

While I can't predict the future, I know that our people and open source will remain vital.

Our industry is cyclical, and we must advocate for open source technology.

I focused on creating a stats system that shows rolling windows of data to track progress.

We’re building for the long term, and now we have statistics with 20 years of history.

There’s a lot more to do, and I can’t wait to see what a “20 Years of Automattic” post says. I’m a lucky guy.

I’m excited to continue building alongside talented individuals.
