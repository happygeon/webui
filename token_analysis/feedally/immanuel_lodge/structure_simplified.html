Immanuel Wilderness Lodge

Best Accommodation in Windhoek | #1 on Tripadvisor

Located minutes from Windhoek, Immanuel Wilderness Lodge offers tranquility in the Namibian Savannah.

Renowned for fantastic food, the lodge is owned by German expatriates Sabine and Stephan, an award-winning chef.

This family-run lodge is ideal for exploring the wilderness, featuring traditional thatched roofs and nine comfortable rooms with ensuite bathrooms.

Enjoy the refreshing pool, hearty meals, and daily bush activities.

For a true Namibian experience with personal service, choose Immanuel Wilderness Lodge.
